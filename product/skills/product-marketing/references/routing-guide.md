# Product Marketing — Routing Guide

Router for product marketing work. Routes to specialized skills.

## Child Skills

| Child | Owns |
|-------|------|
| `buyer-psychology` | Buyer motivation mapping, objection handling frameworks |
| `cro` | Conversion rate optimization, landing page experiments |
| `content-led` | Content-led growth strategy |
| `lead-magnets` | Lead magnet design and copy |

## When to Use This Skill Directly

- Writing product positioning statements.
- Developing messaging hierarchies.
- Crafting product launch communications.
- Competitive differentiation work.

## Routing Decision Tree

```
Is this about understanding why buyers buy or don't buy?
  → buyer-psychology

Is this about improving conversion on a page or flow?
  → cro

Is this about attracting buyers through content?
  → content-led

Is this about creating a downloadable asset to capture leads?
  → lead-magnets

Is this about writing product messaging or positioning?
  → handle directly
```

## Product Marketing Principles

- **Positioning before messaging**: Nail the positioning (who it's for, what it does, why it's different) before writing copy.
- **Voice of customer**: Use exact customer language from interviews in copy — don't paraphrase.
- **One message per page**: Each page/asset owns one core message. Secondary messages dilute.
- **Proof over claims**: Replace "powerful" and "best-in-class" with specific metrics and customer proof.
- **Sales alignment**: Product marketing owns the narrative; sales owns delivery. Align weekly.
