# Example: Content-Led Growth Strategy for a Developer Tool

Context: "Acme CI" — a CI/CD tool for small engineering teams. Currently 80% of signups come from paid ads (CAC rising). Goal: build a content engine that compounds and lowers blended CAC over 2 quarters.

## Strategic thesis

Developers don't want to be sold to; they want to solve a problem and discover the tool while doing it. Win by ranking for high-intent "how do I" problems adjacent to CI/CD, then convert with a fast self-serve trial.

## ICP-to-intent mapping

| ICP pain | Search intent cluster | Funnel stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flaky pipelines | "how to debug flaky tests", "retry failed CI jobs" | TOFU → MOFU |
| Slow builds | "speed up docker build CI", "cache dependencies github actions" | MOFU |
| Migrating CI | "migrate from jenkins to <x>", "github actions vs <x>" | BOFU |

## Content pillars (each owns a cluster)

1. **Pipeline reliability** — 8 articles, one pillar guide. Lead magnet: flaky-test triage checklist.
2. **Build speed** — 6 how-tos with copy-paste config. Lead magnet: build-time calculator.
3. **Migration** — 4 comparison/migration guides. CTA: guided trial.

## Cadence & format

- 2 deep technical how-tos per week, code-first, reproducible.
- Each post ends with a contextual product CTA, not a banner.
- Repurpose each pillar into a newsletter issue + 3 social posts.

## Distribution

- SEO is the compounding channel; seed each post in 1-2 relevant communities (no spam).
- Internal links from high-traffic posts to BOFU migration guides.

## Measurement (review every 4 weeks)

| Metric | Baseline | 90-day target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organic sessions | 4k/mo | 12k/mo |
| Organic → trial CVR | 1.2% | 2.0% |
| Blended CAC | $420 | $300 |
| Assisted signups from content | 8% | 30% |

## First 30 days

Ship the pipeline-reliability pillar + 4 cluster posts, instrument attribution, and launch the flaky-test checklist lead magnet.
