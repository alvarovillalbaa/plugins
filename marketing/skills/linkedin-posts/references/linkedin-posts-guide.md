# LinkedIn Posts Guide

Reference for drafting and adapting LinkedIn feed posts, carousels, and founder updates. The feed rewards a strong hook, native value, and conversation. Pull author voice and positioning from repo-local voice docs.

## How the LinkedIn feed works (practical model)

- The first ~140 characters show before "…see more." That preview decides whether anyone expands the post — it's the whole ballgame.
- Early engagement in the first 60–90 minutes (especially **comments and reshares**) drives how far the post spreads. Dwell time (people stopping to read) also matters.
- **Outbound links in the post body suppress reach** — the algorithm keeps users on-platform. Put links in the first comment or after the hook, and tell readers where to find them.
- Reposting native content (carousels, text, document posts) outperforms link posts.

## Post formats

| Format | Best for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Text post** | Quick insight, story, hot take | 1–3 short paragraphs; whitespace-heavy |
| **Carousel (document)** | Step-by-step, frameworks, lists | High dwell + saves; 6–12 slides |
| **Founder update** | Building-in-public, milestones, learnings | Personal, transparent, specific numbers |
| **Image post** | Behind-the-scenes, screenshots, results | Native image lifts reach |
| **Poll** | Quick engagement, soft research | Low effort, decent reach |
| **Video** | Demos, talking-head insight | Native upload + captions |

## Hook patterns (the first line)

The hook is 80% of the work. Strong patterns:
- **Contrarian**: "Most onboarding advice is wrong. Here's what actually works."
- **Result/number**: "We cut churn 40% in 6 weeks. Three changes did it."
- **Story open**: "A customer almost fired us last month. What they said changed our roadmap."
- **Curiosity gap**: "The feature we almost killed is now 60% of revenue."
- **Direct value**: "Steal this 5-step framework for X."
- **Mistake/confession**: "I wasted $50k learning this. You don't have to."

Hook rules: be specific (numbers, stakes), create an open loop the reader needs to close, never bury it under context. One idea per hook.

## Body structure

- **One line per idea**, generous whitespace — the feed is read on mobile, scanning fast.
- Short. Most high-performing text posts are under ~200 words.
- Build tension → payoff. Deliver on the hook's promise.
- For lists/frameworks, use line breaks or a carousel rather than dense paragraphs.
- Close with a **takeaway** + a **question** that invites comments ("What's worked for you?").

## Carousels

- Slide 1 = the hook (title + promise). It's the thumbnail; make it stop the scroll.
- One idea per slide, big readable text, minimal words.
- Slides 2–N: the steps/points, building momentum.
- Final slide: recap + CTA (follow, comment, save).
- Saves and dwell time make carousels reach far — design for "I'll come back to this."

## Engagement triggers

- **End with a question** — comments are the strongest reach signal.
- **Reply to every early comment** (first hour) — replies count as engagement and signal the post is alive.
- **Tag relevant people sparingly** and only when genuinely relevant (over-tagging is penalized).
- **3–5 specific hashtags** max, mixing niche and broad.
- **Post when your audience is on** (typically weekday mornings in their timezone); consistency beats perfect timing.
- **Hook a discussion, not just applause** — opinions and tradeoffs earn comments; pure positivity earns passive likes.

## Founder updates / build-in-public

- Lead with a real number or a real struggle — transparency is the differentiator.
- Share the lesson, not just the win. Failures and pivots out-engage humble-brags.
- Make it useful to the reader, not just a status report ("here's what we learned" > "here's what we did").
- Consistency compounds — a weekly update builds a following over months.

## Voice & quality

- First person, specific, opinionated. Run through the `humanizing` lane to remove AI cadence.
- No fabricated metrics, customers, or stories. Use real, approved specifics.
- Match the author's voice doc — tone, vocabulary, signature phrasing.
- Cut every line that doesn't earn its place; whitespace is a feature.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] First line works as a standalone hook (specific, open loop).
- [ ] No link in the body (link in first comment instead).
- [ ] Mobile-formatted: short lines, whitespace.
- [ ] Delivers on the hook's promise.
- [ ] Ends with a comment-driving question.
- [ ] Specifics are real and approved.
- [ ] Voice matches; AI tells removed.
