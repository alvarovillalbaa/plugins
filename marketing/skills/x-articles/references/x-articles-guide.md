# X Articles & Long-Form Guide

Reference for drafting long-form X (Twitter) content: Articles, expanded threads, and article-style posts. X rewards a scroll-stopping hook and momentum between units (tweets/paragraphs). Pull author voice from repo-local voice docs.

## Long-form options on X

| Format | What it is | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Thread** | Series of linked posts | Step-by-step, stories, frameworks — the workhorse long-form unit |
| **Long post** (Premium) | Single post beyond 280 chars | Cohesive essay-style piece without thread mechanics |
| **X Article** (Premium/verified) | Formatted long-form with rich text, headers, images | Durable, in-depth pieces; more like a blog post |

Choose: threads for momentum and reach (each tweet is a re-entry point), long posts/articles for cohesive depth and a cleaner read.

## The hook (post 1 / opening)

The first post is everything — it's what appears in the feed and decides reach.
- **Make a specific, bold promise or claim.** "I analyzed 100 cold emails. Here's the 3-line formula that got replies."
- **Open a loop** the reader must close.
- **Lead with the payoff or the tension**, never context or "a thread 🧵" alone (though a thread indicator helps).
- Numbers, stakes, and specificity beat vague intrigue.
- Keep it self-contained: someone who sees only post 1 should want post 2.

## Thread construction

- **One idea per post.** Each tweet should be a complete, valuable beat.
- **Momentum between posts** — end posts with mini-cliffhangers or "but here's the part most people miss…" to pull readers down.
- **Front-load value** — best points early; reach decays down the thread, so don't save the gold for post 12.
- **Length**: 5–15 posts is the common sweet spot. Long enough for substance, short enough to finish. Quality per post > count.
- **Readable units**: short lines, line breaks, one thought per line within a post.
- **Visuals**: images, charts, or screenshots in some posts boost engagement and dwell.
- **Recap + CTA** in the final post: summarize, then ask for a follow, a repost of post 1, or a reply.

## X Article / long post structure

For cohesive long-form (less thread-mechanics):
1. **Title/hook** — specific and curiosity-driven.
2. **Opening** — the stakes or the surprising claim, immediately.
3. **Body** — short paragraphs, subheads (in Articles), one idea per block, building an argument.
4. **Evidence** — concrete examples, numbers, specifics.
5. **Takeaway + CTA** — the one thing to remember, then the ask.

## Engagement mechanics

- **Reposts/quotes drive reach** — design post 1 to be repostable on its own.
- **Replies count heavily** — ask a question; reply to early responders quickly to keep the conversation alive.
- **Bookmarks** signal lasting value (frameworks/how-tos earn these) and quietly boost ranking.
- **The "reply with link" pattern** — keep external links out of post 1 (links suppress reach); put the link in a reply and point readers there.
- **Engagement in the first hour** sets the trajectory — post when your audience is active and seed early replies.
- **Hooks that invite disagreement or strong agreement** out-engage neutral statements.

## Voice & quality

- Punchy, direct, conversational — X is faster and less formal than LinkedIn.
- First person, opinionated, specific. Strong take > balanced summary.
- Run drafts through `humanizing` — X especially punishes corporate/AI cadence.
- No fabricated stats, screenshots, or stories. Real, approved specifics only.
- Match the author's voice doc.

## Repurposing into X long-form

- A blog post → a thread (one section per post) or an X Article.
- A framework → a numbered thread, one step per post.
- Coordinate with `repurposing` for the atomic breakdown and `syndication` for canonical/links back to the source.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Post 1 is a standalone, scroll-stopping hook (specific promise, open loop).
- [ ] No external link in post 1 (link in a reply).
- [ ] Best value is front-loaded; each post earns the next.
- [ ] One idea per post; short readable lines.
- [ ] Ends with a recap + one CTA (follow / repost / reply).
- [ ] Specifics are real and approved.
- [ ] Voice matches; AI tells removed.
