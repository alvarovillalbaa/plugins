Use skills: **product-development**, **research**, and **grill**.

Optional external chain: **office-hours** if installed.

1. **Frame the decision** - Identify the idea, customer, wedge, timeline, and decision the user needs to make.
2. **Test demand reality** - Ask direct questions about customer pain, urgency, alternatives, willingness to pay, and distribution.
3. **Stress the wedge** - Examine why this can start small, win a beachhead, and compound.
4. **Surface risks** - Call out hidden assumptions, market timing, founder fit, execution gaps, and unclear proof.
5. **Return next decision** - Provide the strongest current answer, unresolved questions, and the next validation action.
