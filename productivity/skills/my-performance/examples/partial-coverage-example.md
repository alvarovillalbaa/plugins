# Partial Coverage Example

## Frame

- Role: engineering manager
- Period: 2026-01-01 through 2026-03-31
- Rubric: delivery 40%, team development 35%, stakeholder communication 25%
- Scale: 1 to 4 with declared behavioral anchors

## Evidence

Delivery has an authoritative release log. Stakeholder communication has two dated sources: a project update archive and written partner feedback. Team development has only one undated self-report.

## Correct assessment

Score delivery and stakeholder communication if their evidence maps directly to the rubric anchors. Mark team development `not evaluated`; do not assign zero. Weighted coverage is 65%, so omit the overall score even if the two criterion scores are strong.

Report the covered strengths and gaps, name the missing team-development evidence, and define a next-period commitment to collect dated one-on-one outcomes or feedback. Do not inspect HR systems or save the evaluation to memory without explicit approval.
