# Post-run Check

Before returning an improvement plan:

1. Confirm that every material claim has a source and date or an explicit unknown-date marker.
2. Confirm that facts, inferences, and not-evaluated areas are visibly distinct.
3. Remove any diagnosis or inference about protected or sensitive traits.
4. Limit the plan to three leverage points and ensure every experiment has a measure, guardrail, review date, and stop rule.
5. Confirm that no private connector was accessed without explicit authorization.
6. Confirm that memory candidates remain drafts unless the user separately approved a write.
