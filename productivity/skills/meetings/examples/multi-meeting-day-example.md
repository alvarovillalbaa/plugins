# Multi-meeting Day Example

## Situation

The user provides three meeting records: a 09:00 roadmap decision, a 09:30 customer call, and a 14:00 launch review. The roadmap and customer call overlap. The launch review depends on the roadmap decision. Two records assign action `A-17` to different owners.

## Good portfolio behavior

Prioritize the roadmap decision because it unblocks the launch review, but flag the customer-call overlap rather than moving either event. Surface the participant or delegation decision the user must make. Keep both variants of `A-17`, cite their source meetings, and ask the user to resolve the owner conflict.

Prepare separate briefs and agendas, then roll confirmed decisions and actions into one portfolio. Mark any action with no owner or due date unresolved. Draft the customer follow-up and a proposed calendar change, but do not send or reschedule anything.

Propose the roadmap decision as a memory candidate only if it is durable. Show the candidate and provenance and leave memory unchanged until separately approved.
