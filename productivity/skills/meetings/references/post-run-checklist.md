# Post-run Check

Before returning meeting work:

1. Confirm that every decision and action retains its source meeting.
2. Confirm that every action has one owner and due date or is visibly marked unresolved.
3. Confirm that notes, decisions, actions, and open questions remain separate.
4. Check schedule, decision, dependency, ownership, and deadline conflicts across the portfolio.
5. Keep follow-ups and calendar changes as drafts unless the exact external action was approved.
6. Confirm recording or transcription was neither started nor implied without explicit approval and participant-policy checks.
7. Leave durable memory unchanged unless a separate write was approved.
