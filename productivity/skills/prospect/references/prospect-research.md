# Prospect Research Reference

Reference for turning heterogeneous inputs (LinkedIn, company site, job boards, research) into a decision-ready GTM brief covering Account, Persona, and Next-best-motion. Apply provided scorecards (ICP, ICA, signals) as authoritative rubrics when supplied. Output is an internal brief — outreach itself is human-gated and owned by `sales`.

## Goal

Produce a brief that lets a seller decide, in two minutes: **is this worth pursuing, who do I talk to, and what's the opening?** Every section ends in a decision, not a data dump.

## Inputs and what each yields

| Source | What to extract |
| --- | --- |
| **Company website** | Positioning, products, target market, pricing model, recent launches, customers/logos, tech hints |
| **LinkedIn (company)** | Headcount, growth trend, locations, recent posts, hiring focus |
| **LinkedIn (people)** | Decision-makers, roles, tenure, background, mutual connections, what they post about |
| **Job boards / careers page** | Hiring signals (which teams growing → priorities), tech stack (from JD requirements), pain (roles they're filling) |
| **News / funding (Crunchbase, press)** | Funding stage/recency, expansion, leadership changes, M&A — timing triggers |
| **Product/tech detectors** | Stack, tools in use → integration fit and competitive displacement angles |
| **Reviews / communities** | Real pain points, competitor gripes, language customers use |

## Account analysis

Assess fit and timing:
- **Firmographics** — industry, size (headcount/revenue), geography, business model.
- **Technographics** — current stack, tools, platforms (fit + displacement angles).
- **ICP fit** — score against the provided ICP scorecard if given; otherwise against known firmographic/technographic targets. State fit as strong / moderate / weak with reasons.
- **Timing signals** (the "why now") — funding, hiring surges, leadership changes, product launches, expansion, public pain, contract-renewal windows, regulatory shifts.
- **Account health/size of prize** — rough deal potential and expansion headroom.

## Persona analysis

Identify and profile the people who matter:
- **Map the buying group**: economic buyer (owns budget), champion (feels the pain, advocates internally), end users, technical evaluator, blockers.
- **For the key persona(s)**: role, scope, tenure, what they're measured on, likely priorities and pains, what they post/care about.
- **Score against ICA** (ideal customer/account persona) scorecard if provided.
- **Find the wedge** — the specific person whose problem your product most directly solves; that's the entry point.
- **Connection paths** — mutual connections, shared background, warm-intro routes.

## Signal scoring

When scorecards (ICP / ICA / signals) are provided, treat them as the **authoritative rubric** — apply them literally, score each dimension, and show the scoring. When not provided, use these default signal tiers:
- **Strong (act now)**: recent funding, rapid relevant hiring, public statement of the exact pain, leadership change in the buyer role, using a competitor up for renewal.
- **Moderate (worth a touch)**: ICP-fit firmographics, relevant tech stack, steady growth, engaged on relevant topics.
- **Weak (deprioritize)**: poor fit, no timing trigger, wrong size, no reachable champion.

## Next-best-motion

Translate research into a recommended action:
- **Pursue / nurture / pass** verdict with rationale.
- **Entry point** — which persona to approach first and why.
- **The angle** — the specific hook tying their signal/pain to your value (the "why you, why now").
- **Channel** — email / LinkedIn / X / warm intro, based on where the persona is reachable.
- **Proof to lead with** — the most relevant case study, stat, or capability for this account.
- **Anticipated objection** — the likely pushback and how to preempt it.

## Brief structure (output)

1. **Snapshot** — company, size, stage, ICP-fit verdict, one-line "why now."
2. **Account** — firmographics, technographics, timing signals, fit score.
3. **Persona** — buying group map, key persona profile(s), wedge, connection paths.
4. **Signals & scores** — applied scorecards, ranked signals.
5. **Next best motion** — verdict, entry point, angle, channel, proof, objection.
6. **Sources** — where each claim came from (so a seller can verify).

## Quality gates

- **Cite sources** — every claim traces to an input; a seller must be able to verify.
- **No fabrication** — don't invent headcount, funding, stack, or quotes. Mark unknowns as gaps.
- **Recency** — note dates; a 2-year-old funding round isn't a "why now." Verify current state.
- **Decision-ready** — end in a recommendation, not a profile. If the verdict is "pass," say so and why.
- **Privacy** — use only public/authorized data; treat compiled research as confidential; don't paste into tools that publish or cache.
- **Hand to `sales`** for the actual outreach — this brief informs the motion; it doesn't send it.
