---
name: competitor-scan
description: Run a focused competitor or market-moves scan using productivity research skills.
argument-hint: "[market, segment, or competitor set]"
allowed-tools: [Read, Write, AskUserQuestion, Skill]
---

Use skill: **market-competitor-research** from the productivity plugin. If only the research router is installed, use **research** with the competitor lane.

1. **Set the scan scope** – Ask for the market, customer segment, or named competitors if the request is vague.
2. **Confirm the window** – Default to the last 7 days unless the user specifies another period.
3. **Run the scan** – Follow the competitor research lane to identify new entrants, feature moves, and why they matter.
4. **Add strategic response** – For each finding, include the recommended response or implication.
5. **Deliver** – Output a concise findings report with evidence and the next strategic question.
