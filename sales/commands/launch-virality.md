Use skills: **launches** and **virality**. Chain to Marketing **x-posts**, **images**, or **video** for the channel assets they own.

1. **Gather evidence** — Resolve the product, audience, approved positioning, source material, claims, launch objective, channel constraints, and success metric. Mark missing proof instead of inventing it.
2. **Choose the launch angle** — Identify the concrete outcome, credible proof, tension, or point of view. Controversy and incentives are optional tactics, not requirements; reject deceptive or manipulative engagement bait.
3. **Research the channel** — Use current evidence when trend or competitor research matters. Record dates and sources, and distinguish structural patterns from copied wording.
4. **Draft campaign assets** — Produce a primary post or thread plus only the variants and reply starters justified by the brief. Route channel copy through Marketing `x-posts` and visual assets through their canonical owners.
5. **Plan distribution** — Define phases, owned and partner channels, timing assumptions, responsible owners, approval gates, and stop criteria. Do not assume influencer access, paid spend, giveaways, or a fixed reply volume.
6. **Score and revise** — Evaluate specificity, novelty, clarity, proof, audience fit, and CTA strength. Report trade-offs; do not invent a universal perfect score.
7. **Deliver** — Return positioning, final drafts, distribution plan, measurement plan, approval queue, and unsupported claims that still need evidence.

## Boundary

This command coordinates a launch campaign. Use Marketing `x-post` for one organic post and `x-engage` for public replies or quote posts.
