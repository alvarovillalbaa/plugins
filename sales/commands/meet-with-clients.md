Use skills: **technical-sales**, **go-to-market**, **follow-up**, and **outreach**. Chain to **productivity/research** only when preparation requires new evidence.

1. **Prepare account context** - Gather the client, opportunity stage, attendees, prior notes, constraints, and desired outcome.
2. **Shape the agenda** - Define discovery questions, demo/proof points, objections to test, and decision criteria.
3. **Run the meeting brief** - Produce a concise talk track and note-taking structure.
4. **Plan follow-up** - Draft next-step emails, mutual action items, collateral requests, and CRM notes.
5. **Deliver artifacts** - Return agenda, prep notes, discovery prompts, follow-up copy, and open risks.

## Boundary

This command owns a sales or customer meeting lifecycle. General internal meeting portfolios belong to the Productivity `meetings` skill.
