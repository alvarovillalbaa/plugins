## Rules for All Templates

- Subject line: specific (not "Quick question" or "Following up")
- Opening line: about them, not you
- Value proposition: one sentence, one metric
- CTA: one ask, low commitment ("20-minute call", "useful to see a 2-pager?")
- Length: under 100 words
- No attachments on cold outreach
- Personalize the opening line for every send — never batch-send a generic template
