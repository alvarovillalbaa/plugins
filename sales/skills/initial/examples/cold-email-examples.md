# First-Touch Outreach Examples

Five cold email examples for different ICP segments. Each is under 100 words.

---

## Example 1: Engineering Leader at a Series B SaaS

**Subject**: Code review bottleneck at [Company]

Hi [Name],

Noticed your team grew from 12 to 28 engineers last year. At that scale, PR review backlogs tend to become a real drag on velocity.

We help engineering teams cut average review cycle time by 60% using automated AI review on every PR — without replacing human reviewers.

Worth a 20-minute call to see if there's a fit?

[Your Name]

## Example 2: Founder-Led Company (No Clear Engineering Title)

**Subject**: Question about your deployment workflow

One thing that often causes problems at that cadence: manual QA gates that slow down the last 20%. We've built tooling that automates the pre-deploy checks most teams do by hand.

Would a quick look make sense?

## Example 4: Technical Founder (Product-Led Company)

Looked at your GitHub (public repos) — love the architecture. One thing I noticed: your CI pipeline runs tests sequentially, which looks like it's adding ~12 minutes per build.

We can typically cut that to under 4 minutes. Not a sales pitch — just thought it was worth flagging.

If you're ever interested in a conversation about CI/CD optimization, I'm happy to share what we've seen.

[Your Name]
