# Go-To-Market — Routing Guide

Router for GTM strategy and execution. Routes to specialist sales skills.

## Child Skills

| Child | Owns |
|-------|------|
| `first-customers` | Founder-led sales, design partners, early traction |
| `technical-sales` | Technical discovery, POC, technical evaluation |
| `lead-signals` | Timely prospect evidence, Signals database capture, People/Companies relations and filtered record views |
| `revenue-intelligence` | Pipeline analysis, deal signals, forecast |

## When to Use This Skill Directly

- Designing the overall GTM motion (PLG vs sales-led vs channel).
- Choosing target market segment for initial GTM.
- Structuring the GTM team and handoffs.
- Crafting the GTM strategy document.

## GTM Motion Decision Tree

```
Is the request about finding and recording prospect or lead signals?
  → Use: lead-signals
  → Chain to prospect only when a full account/persona brief is needed

Is the product self-serve with a free tier?
  → Product-Led Growth (PLG) motion
  → Use: first-customers for initial early adopters, then product-led expansion

Is the product sold to technical buyers or requires POC?
  → Technical Sales-Led motion
  → Use: technical-sales for discovery and evaluation

Is the ACV > $50K?
  → Enterprise Sales-Led motion
  → Use: first-customers (warm intros) → technical-sales → revenue-intelligence

Is this an existing product entering a new market?
  → Channel or partnership motion
  → Use: go-to-market directly for partner strategy
```

## GTM Principles

- **ICP before market**: Define ICP before defining the market. Know who you're selling to first.
- **Repeatability before scale**: Nail the first 10 customers manually before building sales processes.
- **Land and expand**: Design the initial land to make expansion natural — don't optimize for one-time sale.
- **Feedback loops**: GTM learns from win/loss. Instrument it from day one.
- **Competitive differentiation**: Know why you beat (and lose to) each competitor before hiring sales.
