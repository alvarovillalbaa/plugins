# Outbound Sequence Example: Engineering Leader (6-Touch, 21 Days)

**ICP**: VP Engineering / Head of Engineering at Series B+ SaaS, 15–100 engineers  
**Goal**: Book a discovery call  
**Channels**: Email (T1, T3, T5, T6), LinkedIn (T2, T4)

---

## Touch 1 — Email (Day 1)

**Subject**: Code review bottleneck at {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Noticed {{company}} grew your engineering team significantly recently. At that scale, PR review backlogs tend to become a real drag on velocity.

We help engineering teams cut average review cycle time by 60% using AI review on every PR — without replacing human reviewers.

Worth a 20-minute call?

[Name]

---

## Touch 2 — LinkedIn Connection (Day 3)

**Connection Note** (300 chars max):

Hi {{first_name}} — reached out by email about PR review cycle time. Connecting here too in case email isn't your preferred channel. Happy to share more context if useful.

---

## Touch 3 — Email (Day 6)

**Subject**: RE: Code review bottleneck at {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Following up on my note from last week.

Quick context on what I'm seeing at similar companies: teams at 20–40 engineers typically have 2–4 day average cycle times. After implementing AI review, that drops to under 12 hours.

If cycle time isn't a priority right now, no worries — just want to make sure it's on your radar.

Worth 20 minutes?

[Name]

---

## Touch 4 — LinkedIn Message (Day 10)

Hi {{first_name}}, saw your post on [recent LinkedIn activity if available]. Wanted to reach out directly — sent a couple of emails about reducing PR cycle time. If email isn't the right channel, happy to chat here or share a 2-pager. No pressure either way.

---

## Touch 5 — Email (Day 15)

**Subject**: One resource before I stop

Hi {{first_name}},

Last note from me for a while.

Before I stop, I wanted to share this [case study / benchmark / short video] — it's the clearest example of what we've built.

[Link]

If the timing is ever right, you know where to find me.

[Name]

---

## Touch 6 — Email (Day 21) — Breakup

**Subject**: Closing the loop

Hi {{first_name}},

I've reached out a few times without a response — totally understand if the timing is off or this isn't a priority.

I'll stop reaching out for now. If anything changes on your end — team scaling, review backlog, anything — feel free to reply and I'll pick it back up.

Thanks for your time.

[Name]

---

## Sequence Rules

- Stop immediately on any reply (yes or no).
- Never send T5 and T6 on the same day.
- Pause the sequence if the prospect opens any email 3+ times (warm signal — call instead).
- Wait 6 months before re-enrolling in a new sequence.
