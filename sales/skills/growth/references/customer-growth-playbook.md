# Customer Growth Playbook

Reference for customer growth: retention, expansion, churn-risk review, lifecycle nudges, and customer-success GTM. The cheapest revenue is the customer you already have — net revenue retention compounds harder than new logos. Pull product/ICP facts from repo-local docs; all customer outreach is human-gated.

## The retention-expansion engine

Net Revenue Retention (NRR) = (starting revenue + expansion − contraction − churn) / starting revenue. NRR > 100% means the existing base grows even with zero new logos. Growth motion priorities:
1. **Prevent churn** (defense) — keep the revenue you have.
2. **Drive expansion** (offense) — upsell/cross-sell/seat growth.
3. **Earn advocacy** — references, referrals, case studies (feed `sales` and `marketing`).

## Account health signals

Score accounts to know where to act. Composite of:

| Signal category | Healthy | At risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product usage** | Active, growing, broad feature adoption | Declining logins, narrow usage, dormant seats |
| **Engagement** | Responsive, attends QBRs, champions active | Ghosting, no-shows, champion left |
| **Outcomes** | Hitting their goals with the product | Not seeing value / ROI unclear |
| **Commercial** | On time, expanding, multi-year | Downgrade requests, payment issues, month-to-month |
| **Sentiment** | High NPS/CSAT, positive support | Complaints, escalations, low scores |
| **Relationship** | Multi-threaded across the org | Single-threaded on one contact |

Combine into a simple health score (green/yellow/red). **Single-threading** and **champion departure** are the most under-weighted churn predictors — flag them loudly.

## Churn-risk review

1. **Detect early** — usage decline and engagement drop precede cancellation by weeks/months. Watch leading indicators, not just the renewal date.
2. **Diagnose the type**:
   - *Value churn* — not getting outcomes → re-onboard, prove ROI.
   - *Relationship churn* — champion left / single-threaded → re-multithread fast.
   - *Fit churn* — wrong customer for the product → manage gracefully, learn for ICP.
   - *Competitive churn* — being displaced → competitive save play (battlecard).
   - *Budget churn* — economic → flexible terms, downgrade-to-retain vs. lose.
3. **Run the save play** — tailored to type: re-onboarding, exec alignment, ROI review, roadmap commitment, or commercial flexibility.
4. **Time it to the renewal cycle** — start renewal motions 60–90 days out, not at expiry.

## Expansion plays

Expansion should follow value, not precede it. Triggers and motions:

| Expansion trigger | Play |
| --- | --- |
| Hitting usage/seat limits | Upsell capacity/tier |
| Adopted core, not adjacent features | Cross-sell modules |
| New team/department interest | Land-and-expand to new group |
| Strong outcomes achieved | Tier upgrade with ROI proof |
| New use case emerges | Map product to the new need |
| Org growth (hiring) | Seat expansion |

Rules: expand from a position of delivered value (a happy customer expands; a struggling one churns). Multi-thread before expanding (new buyers for new budget). Tie the ask to *their* growth, not your quota.

## Lifecycle nudges

Map touches to the customer journey so engagement is proactive, not reactive:
- **Onboarding (0–90 days)** — fastest path to first value; the highest-leverage retention window. Most churn is set here.
- **Adoption** — drive feature breadth and habit; celebrate milestones.
- **Value realization** — QBRs/check-ins that quantify outcomes vs. their goals.
- **Renewal** — start early, lead with delivered value, remove friction.
- **Advocacy** — turn delighted customers into references, referrals, and case studies.

Nudges should be useful to the customer (tip, milestone, relevant feature), not just check-ins. Personalize to their usage and goals.

## Customer-success GTM

- **Segment by potential and risk** — high-touch for high-value/high-risk, tech-touch/automated for the long tail.
- **Define success per segment** — what "healthy" and "expanded" look like.
- **Instrument** — health scores, usage data, renewal forecasts feed the motion (coordinate with `revenue-intelligence`/`revenue-ops`).
- **Close the loop to product/ICP** — churn reasons and feature gaps feed `product` discovery and `system` ICP refinement.

## Quality gates

- **Lead with the customer's outcome**, not your renewal/quota.
- **No pushy expansion** before value is delivered — it accelerates churn.
- **Real data** — health scores and ROI claims use actual usage/results, not guesses.
- **Human-gated** — outreach, commercial offers, and downgrade/save terms need approval and `finances` coordination.
- **Confidential** — treat customer usage and account data as sensitive.

## Handoffs

- Pipeline/forecasting and health instrumentation → `revenue-intelligence`, `revenue-ops`, `sales-pipeline`.
- Save/competitive plays → `collateral` (battlecards), `commercial-docs`.
- Advocacy assets → `marketing` (case studies), `first-customers` (references).
- Product gaps and ICP learnings → `product`, `system` (`icp`).
