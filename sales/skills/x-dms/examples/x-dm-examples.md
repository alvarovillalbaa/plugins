# X DM Examples

Five worked examples for different signal types. X DMs work best when you reference a specific public action the prospect took.

---

## Example 1: After They Post About a Problem You Solve

**Trigger**: Prospect tweeted "our PR cycle time is out of control, 4+ days average"

**DM**:

Hey [Name] — saw your post about PR cycle time. We solved this for a few teams at your stage. The short version: automated pre-review on every PR before it hits a human reviewer. Gets the average under 12 hours. Worth a quick chat?

*(84 words — tight, specific, one CTA)*

---

## Example 2: After They Reply to an Industry Thread

**Trigger**: Prospect left a smart reply in a thread about CI/CD optimization

**DM**:

Hey [Name] — your point about parallelization in [thread] was exactly right. We've been working on the same problem from a different angle. Would love to swap notes for 20 minutes if you're open to it.

*(37 words — no hard sell, positions as peer conversation)*

---

## Example 3: After They Share Your Content

**Trigger**: Prospect retweeted your article or liked a thread you wrote

**DM**:

Hey [Name] — thanks for sharing the post. Curious: is [problem area the post was about] something you're actively working on at [Company]? Happy to share what we've seen from similar teams if useful.

*(38 words — converts a passive signal into a conversation opener)*

---

## Example 4: After Following Up a Cold Email

**Trigger**: Sent 2–3 cold emails, no response, prospect active on X

**DM**:

Hey [Name] — I've sent a couple emails but X might work better. Reaching out about [specific problem]. Would 15 minutes make sense? No pressure if the timing's off.

*(32 words — acknowledges the email, offers an out, keeps it short)*

---

## Example 5: Re-engaging a Warm Prospect Who Went Cold

**Trigger**: Had a call 3 months ago, they went quiet, now they're tweeting again

**DM**:

Hey [Name] — we spoke back in [month] about [topic]. Saw you're posting about [related thing]. Wondering if anything's changed on your end — happy to pick up where we left off if timing is better now.

*(40 words — warm tone, references the prior relationship, no pressure)*

---

## What Makes a Good X DM

1. **Reference something specific** they did publicly. "I saw your post on X" > "I found you on X."
2. **One question or one ask** — not both.
3. **Under 50 words** for opener. You can expand in follow-up messages.
4. **No attachments or links** in the first message — feels spammy.
5. **Don't DM if they have DMs disabled** — engage publicly in their replies first.
