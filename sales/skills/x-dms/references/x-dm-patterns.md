# X DM Patterns

Reference for drafting and refining X (Twitter) direct messages and reply-to-DM sales motions. X is the most relationship-first, least formal channel — warm up publicly before you ever DM. Outreach is human-gated; personalize from real signal.

## The X outreach reality

A cold DM to someone who's never heard of you mostly gets ignored or marked spam. X rewards a **public-first** motion: become a familiar name in their replies and timeline, *then* the DM lands as a continuation, not an intrusion. The whole game is earning recognition before the ask.

## The warm-up (do this first)

1. **Follow and pay attention** — know what they post and care about.
2. **Reply with value** — thoughtful, genuine replies to their tweets. Add insight, not "great tweet 🔥". Over days/weeks this builds recognition.
3. **Get on their radar** — they start recognizing your handle. Some interaction (a like-back, a reply) signals you're warm.
4. **Then DM** — now your message references an existing relationship, not a cold start.

Skipping the warm-up is the main reason X DMs fail.

## First DM

- **Reference the relationship/context** — your prior exchange, their content, a shared interest. Continuation, not cold open.
- **Casual and human** — X is informal. A stiff, email-style pitch reads wrong here. Lowercase, conversational.
- **Short** — one to a few lines. X DMs are quick-read.
- **No-ask or soft-ask** — lead with a genuine question or value; save the pitch for after the conversation starts.
- **No links in the first DM** — feels spammy and can trip filters; earn the conversation first.

## Reply-to-DM motion

When someone replies (to your DM or engages with your content):
- **Keep it conversational** — react like a person, not a script. Match their energy and brevity.
- **Build rapport before transitioning** — a couple of genuine exchanges before any business angle.
- **Transition naturally** — connect their interest/pain to what you do only once there's a real thread.
- **Move to the next step lightly** — a call, a resource, a demo — framed as helpful, not a hard close.

## Timing

- DM when they're active (recently posting) — replies are more likely when they're on the app.
- Respond to their engagement promptly while it's fresh.
- Space follow-ups generously; X follow-up spam is very visible and reputation-damaging.

## Follow-up

- **Light touch** — one gentle follow-up if a conversation stalls, then let it rest. X is not an email-cadence channel.
- **Re-engage publicly** instead of repeated DMs — keep showing up in their replies to stay warm.
- **Honor silence** — no response after a nudge means stop DMing; keep the public relationship.

## Voice & quality

- Casual, specific, human — run through `humanizing`; X especially punishes corporate/templated tone.
- **Real context** — reference true interactions/content; fabricated familiarity ("loved your last post" when there wasn't one) backfires fast and is easy to catch.
- **No fabricated proof** — real, approved claims only.
- Match the sender's voice doc.

## Quality gates

- **Public warm-up before cold DMs** — no spray DMing strangers.
- **Respectful, low-volume** — X spam damages reputation publicly; keep it human-scale.
- **No links/pitch in first DM** — earn the conversation.
- **Honor disinterest** — stop on silence/no.
- **Human-gated** — present drafts; a human sends. No automated DMing (violates platform norms).
- **Confidential** — treat prospect data as sensitive.
