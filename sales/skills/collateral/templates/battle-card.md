## Discovery Questions to Ask

Use these to surface competitor weaknesses without attacking directly:

1. "How are you currently handling [pain point]? How long does that take?"
2. "Have you had any issues with [known competitor problem]?"
3. "What's your timeline for deciding? What's driving that date?"

---

## Objection Handling

**"We've used [Competitor] for years — switching is risky."**  
> "That's fair. What's worked well for you? And what's still on your wishlist?"  
> [Let them describe the gap. Map your strengths to their wishlist.]

**"[Competitor] is [cheaper/faster/better known]."**  
> "[Acknowledge]. The difference is [specific differentiator with evidence]."  
> "Would it be useful to see a side-by-side on the use cases you care most about?"

**"[Competitor] says they can do X."**  
> "Let's put it in the POC. If they can do X for your data, go with them."  
> [Confidence, not defensiveness. Offer the proof of concept.]
